# ABSTRACT
This action research aimed to investigate the effects of using Quizlet on improving students’ vocabulary retention. Thirty students of grade 11A1 at Thai Nguyen high school in Thai Nguyen province were chosen. The data was collected by vocabulary tests and questionnaire to answer the research question. The findings showed that Quizlet application had a positive effect on the students’ vocabulary retention. Students used Quizlet application in learning their vocabulary at class or at home and they were interested in applying this application in learning English vocabulary. Data from the vocabulary tests scores and questionnaire demonstrated that Quizlet application helped the 11th-grade students improve their vocabulary retention. Based on the results, the researcher gave the effect of using Quizlet application in students’ vocabulary retention and made some suggestions for teachers and students to apply and use Quizlet application effectively in teaching and learning English lessons in order to improve the students’ word retention.

# 1. Introduction
Vocabulary is the basis for communication and important for language proficiency processes [1]. It is as important as other skills such as listening, speaking, reading, and writing. Alqahtani [2] said that “vocabulary knowledge is often viewed as a critical tool for a second language learner because limited vocabulary in a second language learner impedes successful communication”. By mastering vocabulary, they can deliver messages to others without any miscommunication. Furthermore, students’ proficiency in the English language is also measured by the number of words they have memorized. When students have vocabulary limitations, they find difficulties to improve their English skills. Therefore, vocabulary should be taught directly in the classroom activity.

Teachers who design lesson plans should know the benefits of using Information Computer Technology (ICT) and integrate it into English teaching and learning activity [3]. Smartphones are considered an ICT tool that could increase motivation in learning vocabulary. Moreover, Quizlet is a mobile application widely used for vocabulary learning [5–7].

The study follows the SAMR model (Substitution–Augmentation–Modification–Redefinition) developed by Puentedura [8] to frame technology integration for vocabulary learning.

The context at Thai Nguyen high school:
- Vocabulary learning is not intensive in the official curriculum.  
- Students easily forget new words without extra home practice.  
- Teachers lack time to provide extended word families.  

Thus, the researcher conducted the action research **“Improving students’ vocabulary retention through using Quizlet Application.”**

Word retention is defined as the ability to provide meaning of a new word after a period of time [10], including:
- **Short-term retention**
- **Long-term retention**

**Research question:**  
*How does Quizlet Application improve the students’ vocabulary retention?*

# 2. Methods

## 2.1. Action research design
The study adopted the model of action research by McTaggart & Retallick [9] with four steps:
1. **Planning**  
2. **Action**  
3. **Observation**  
4. **Reflection**

Only one cycle was applied due to time limitations.

## 2.2. Subjects of the study
- 30 students (6 males, 24 females) from class 11A1, Thai Nguyen High School.
- Approximately 16 years old.
- English levels between elementary and pre-intermediate.
- Each had a mobile phone with Wifi/4G for Quizlet access.
- Selected due to technological readiness and class experience.

## 2.3. Data collection instrument
Vocabulary tests and questionnaires were used.

Short-term word retention:
- 24 immediate vocabulary tests after each lesson.

Long-term retention:
- 8 tests after finishing each unit.  
- 1 test one week after intervention.  
- 1 test four weeks after intervention.  

Quizlet **Test mode** was used to create randomized tests with:
- Written answers  
- Multiple-choice  
- Matching  
- True/false  

Incorrect items highlighted in red with correct answers shown.

### 2.3.1. Tests for short-term memory
- Administered immediately after vocabulary lessons.
- Each test: 5 minutes.  
- Format: matching, gap-filling, picture + correct word.

Students also practiced independently through Quizlet modes:
- Writing  
- Testing  
- Matching  
- Spelling  

### 2.3.2. Tests for word retention
- 8 vocabulary tests after all units were finished.
- Format identical to lesson tests.
- 10-minute duration.

Two long-term tests:
- One week after intervention (15 minutes).  
- Four weeks after intervention (15 minutes).  

## 2.4. Data collection procedure
Teaching material: *Tieng Anh 11* (Ministry of Education and Training).

Eight units (Units 8–15) were selected. Vocabulary taught through:
- Reading  
- Writing  
- Listening  

**Intervention lasted 8 weeks (June 15 – Aug 15, 2021).**

### Planning
Teacher prepared Quizlet lesson plans, gained approval, and trained students on use.

### Action
Quizlet applied in three SAMR stages:

#### Substitution
- Students learned new words via flashcards.

#### Augmentation
- Activities included studying, spelling, writing, flashcards.

#### Modification
- Teacher modified flashcards and used **Test Mode** to check vocabulary.

### Observation
Vocabulary tests + questionnaires collected for data analysis.

### Reflection
Researcher analyzed the Quizlet-based instruction and made recommendations.

## 2.5. Data analysis procedure
SPSS was used for:
- Statistical analysis  
- Percentage distribution  
- Tables, charts

# 3. Results and Discussion

## 3.1. Results

### 3.1.1. Comparison of results of the three tests in each unit and after each unit
(Referenced as Figure 1)

Key findings:
- Test scores after each unit were higher than the three initial tests.
- 16.7% excellent scores and 63.3% very good scores in post-unit tests.  
- Zero students achieved excellent scores in initial three tests.  
- Word retention improved significantly.

### 3.1.2. Comparison of results after one week and four weeks
(Referenced as Figure 2)

Findings:
- After one week: 20% excellent, 70% very good, 10% good.
- After four weeks: 25% excellent, 70% very good, 5% good.
- No student fell below “good.”

Shows strong **long-term retention** supported by Quizlet.

## 3.2. Discussion
Quizlet had **positive impacts** on vocabulary retention.

Reasons:
1. Increased motivation—students liked using smartphones for learning.  
2. Reduced boredom—Quizlet activities felt like games.  
3. Competition improved engagement.  
4. Consistent with results of previous studies [11–14].

---

# 4. Conclusion
The study concluded that Quizlet significantly improved:
- students’ vocabulary learning  
- students’ short-term retention  
- students’ long-term retention  

Recommendations:
- Teachers should integrate Quizlet into vocabulary teaching.  
- Students should use Quizlet regularly to reinforce word retention.
